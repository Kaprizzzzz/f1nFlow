# Privacy Policy (Telegram Mini App)

Effective date: 2026-05-01

## Data we collect
- Telegram account identifiers and profile basics used for authentication.
- Financial tracking data entered by users (transactions, categories, goals preferences).
- Product analytics and engagement signals (streaks, badges, app usage timestamps).
- Billing and subscription records for paid Goals Pro features.
- Consent records (policy version + timestamp).

## Data use
- Provide and secure app functionality.
- Prevent fraud and abuse.
- Enable support and operational reliability.

## Retention
- Account/state data: retained until deletion request or account inactivity policy.
- Security audit logs: 180 days minimum.
- Billing/consent records: 3 years minimum.
